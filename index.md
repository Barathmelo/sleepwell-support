## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is my sleep data missing?

SleepWell reads available sleep and health data from Apple Health with your permission. Open Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices to review your permissions. Data availability depends on the devices and apps connected to Apple Health.

### Why did Night Sound Monitoring stop?

By default, monitoring may stop when your iPhone is locked or the app moves to the background. Continue When Locked is available during your included first full-night experience and with Sleep Well Pro. Keep your iPhone connected to power during overnight monitoring.

### Does SleepWell save my audio?

Audio is not saved by default. Night sound analysis runs on your device, and no audio is uploaded. If you enable Save Snore Clips, only detected snore clips are stored locally. Unpinned clips are deleted after 7 days.

### How do I restore my subscription?

Open SleepWell, go to Settings, select Sleep Well Pro, and tap Restore Purchases.

### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iPhone Settings, tap your Apple Account name, then tap Subscriptions.
